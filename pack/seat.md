# The seat this pack trains

**2 of 8** in the pack. Previous: [jargon.md](jargon.md). Next: [briefing.md](briefing.md).

A typical **Director – Global Markets AI** seat in APAC: Hong Kong (or relocate), hybrid team of **4–6**, sitting in a **centralised Quant** group that exists **for Markets**. Front-office aligned. Direct influence on trading outcomes. Not a software-engineering hire parked in CIB Tech.

The bar that should change how you sit every round:

> Everyone needs to be competent at coding and understand the fundamentals of modern GenAI. You do not need to be an expert at all topics. They are not looking for software engineers.

Electronic / high-frequency trading exposure is **advantageous, not essential**. Typical profile: already **Director for ~2–3 years**, now taking a **broader** leadership remit (team + APAC + strategy), still the **senior technical authority**.

## What to prove

| Mandate | Prove it by saying | Do not |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Build and lead a 4–6 AI/ML team for GM | First hires are **modellers / applied scientists who can code**, not a platform-engineering org. Hire less until a desk is using something. | Six MLOps engineers and zero users. |
| End-to-end: ideation → production **and governance** | One FX **PoC** that reaches **shadow/canary** plus a written **tier** and kill switch. | A strategy deck with no artefact. |
| Partner with trading desks and business leadership | Name eFX, eSales, Equities/Prime. Measurable **PnL** (markout **and** fill rate). | “I’ll align with stakeholders.” |
| Work with **engineering** and governance | You **specify** models and information sets; engineering **serves**. Two-speed: research vs engine. | Pretend you will rewrite the pricing engine in Python. |
| Forecasting, recommenders, RL, decision under uncertainty | Those four are the **ML round syllabus**. PnL is the loss, not AUC. | A tour of every architecture you have heard of. |
| **Initial use cases in FX**, PoC, then scale | 90 days = one FX slice. 18 months = Equities/Prime on the same rails. | Boil the ocean, or FX-only forever. |
| Responsible AI and model governance | SS1/23 + HKMA; desk assistant ≠ pricing tier; HITL. | “We’ll be responsible.” |
| Mandate **extremely broad**: quant modelling **and GenAI / agentic** | Two workstreams: (A) statistical FX PoC (B) **agentic** desk-assistant tools. | Only eFX microstructure, or only chatbots. |
| Senior technical authority, hands-on where required | You can still derive purged CV and kill a leaky backtest. You review every information set. | “I used to code.” |
| Drive **AI/LLM initiatives** | Fundamentals of tools, RAG, hallucination, HITL — not being the firm’s LLMOps expert. | Claiming you will fine-tune GPT on the quote stream. |

## Story bank

Prepare **one 90-second STAR** per line.

1. Hands-on AI/ML in a **stakeholder-heavy** shop (desk, risk, or product — not only a lab).
2. A programme you owned **from first meeting through deployment and MRM/governance**.
3. Markets or IB (APAC colour if you have it; if not, say how you will get it in 90 days).
4. A time you engaged **front office / MD** and changed the plan.
5. Already operating at Director: what **broadens** here (APAC, hybrid 4–6, Quant-central, GenAI + modelling).
6. A team you **built or scaled**, including an **LLM/agentic** initiative even if small.
7. If you do **not** have HFT: that is allowed. Show you know when latency matters and when it does not — then **partner** with eFX engineering. Do not fake 50µs war stories.

## How this changes the four rounds

- **ML** — still the core “technical excellence” exam. Forecasting + recommenders + decision-under-uncertainty are **on the syllabus**. RL is listed; treat it as **offline / simulated** unless they push.
- **DL / GenAI** — **agentic** is a mandate word, not a side quest. The desk assistant is the live example. You need **fundamentals**, not every topic.
- **Coding** — **competence**, not a software-engineer screen. Python, information sets, a leaky backtest. Skip kdb/Java/latency-system design unless they ask.
- **Project** — fourth **technical**: a messy live scenario (leakage, metrics, serving, conduct, GenAI as a constraint). Not a 90-day org plan. How you would start the job lives on **90 days**.

Memorise this sentence:

> I will be the senior AI/ML authority for APAC Markets Quant: hands-on on the first models, competent in coding and modern GenAI, working **with** engineering — not as a software-engineering hire, and not as a latency specialist unless the desk needs one later.
